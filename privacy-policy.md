# Privacy Policy for Mega Blaster

**Last updated:** February 2026

Mega Blaster respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Mega Blaster mobile application.

---

## Information Collection and Use

Mega Blaster does **not** collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data.

The app:
- Does not require account creation
- Does not request personal information
- Does not collect location data
- Does not access contacts, photos, or files
- Does not use analytics or tracking services
- Does not include advertising networks

All gameplay data such as scores and progress are stored **locally on your device only**.

---

## Data Storage

Any saved game progress or settings remain entirely on the user's device and are never transmitted externally.

---

## Third-Party Services

Mega Blaster does not use third-party services that collect user data.

---

## Children’s Privacy

Mega Blaster does not knowingly collect any personal information from children. Since no data is collected at all, the app is safe for all age groups.

---

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated in the future if app functionality changes. Any updates will be posted on this page.

---

## Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, you may contact the developer via the GitHub repository associated with this app.
